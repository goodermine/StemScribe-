Aaron cares about commercial-safe licensing of models and dependencies. Default
to MIT / commercial-cleared options; gate anything non-commercial (CC-BY-NC,
e.g. Demucs / `htdemucs_6s`) behind an explicit opt-in, and never substitute one
silently. When a candidate is evaluated and rejected on licence grounds, record
why so nobody re-chases it. This is the same rule aaroncodex already enforces
for separation models ("never silently substitute a non-commercial model").

> evidence: chose the StemScribe separation option "6-stem, commercial-safe (recommended)" — session of 2026-08-09
